Texas Undergraduate Research Conference in the Social Sciences

Join us for the second annual Texas Undergraduate Research Conference in the Social Sciences on Saturday, November 14, 2026. This will be an excellent opportunity for students to showcase their research and engage with peers and experts in the field. Both newcomers and experienced conference attendees are welcome. Sponsored by the University of North Texas Anthropology, Economics, Geography, Political Science, Psychology, and Sociology programs.

Our Offerings

TURCSS is built for undergraduates presenting research, many for the first time. Here is what you take home from the day.

Networking

Meet peers from universities across Texas who are asking the same kinds of questions you are.

  • Undergraduate researchers from multiple universities and social science disciplines
  • Faculty and graduate students from six UNT social science departments
  • Professional relationships that extend beyond the conference

Mentoring

Talk with faculty and graduate students about where your research, and your degree, can take you.

  • Advice on life after graduation, including graduate school and careers in the public and private sectors
  • Conversations with scholars who study the topics you care about

Feedback on Your Research

Get substantive comments on your project from people who do research for a living.

  • Faculty and graduate students respond to paper presentations on each panel
  • Poster presenters hear from attendees and experts throughout the session
  • Ideas to strengthen your thesis, capstone, or next project

Presentation Skills

Practice presenting your work to an academic audience in a supportive setting.

  • Explain your question, methods, and findings clearly and concisely
  • Field questions from faculty, graduate students, and peers
  • First-time presenters are welcome

Paper and Poster Presentations

Share your work in the format that fits your project.

  • Paper presentations on panels with faculty and graduate students
  • Poster presentations with one-on-one conversations about your findings
  • Open to research on any social science topic

Recognition and Closing Ceremony

Showcase your academic achievements to a wider audience and add a conference presentation to your resume.

  • A presentation credit graduate programs and employers recognize
  • Closing ceremony with refreshments after the afternoon sessions
  • Door prize drawings before heading home

Ready to Join Us?

Submit an abstract to present a paper or poster at TURCSS on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the University of North Texas in Denton.

Abstracts are due October 10, 2026.
