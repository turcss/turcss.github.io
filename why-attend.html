Why Attend TURCSS?

Presenting at TURCSS gives undergraduate researchers experience, feedback, and connections that carry into graduate school and careers.

  1. 1

    Present your research

    Gain valuable experience presenting your research findings, network with peers from different universities, and engage in discussions about a wide range of social science topics.

  2. 2

    Build your research and presentation skills

    Strengthen your research and presentation skills, explore new ideas, perspectives, and research methods presented by fellow students, and find inspiration for your future academic and professional work.

  3. 3

    Earn recognition

    Receive recognition for your hard work and showcase your academic achievements to a wider audience. A TURCSS presentation is an excellent addition to your resume.

  4. 4

    Connect with peers

    Meet peers who share your interest in the social sciences and build connections and professional relationships that extend beyond the conference.

  5. 5

    Explore what comes next

    Learn about opportunities after your undergraduate program, including graduate school and careers in the public and private sectors.
